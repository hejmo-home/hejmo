# Hejmo Nordic Calm

Binding values come from `docs/spec.md` ("Design specification Nordic Calm"); the dark palette comes from ADR 0015. The front matter above is the single record of the tokens; `web/static/hejmo.css` implements them as CSS custom properties and `web/design_test.go` fails when a colour token and its CSS custom property differ, when either side lacks the other, or when a contrast drops below 4.5:1. Other tokens (sizes, type, radii) are compared in the same test file by an explicit list.

## Overview

- Calm, plenty of white space, white cards on a warm background, one muted colour per person.
- No gradients, no shadows, no animations on E-Ink. Touch targets are at least 44 px.
- Three palettes, selected with `data-theme` on `<html>` or a container: **light** (default, colour), **dark**, **bw** (black-and-white, for E-Ink black-and-white displays). Dark in black-and-white does not exist.

## Colors

- Token names are `<theme>-<role>`: `light-`, `dark-`, `bw-`. In CSS the prefix is dropped and the theme block overrides the default (`--background`, `--person-1-tint`, ...).
- Roles: `background`, `surface` (columns, tiles, chips), `text`, `text-secondary` (labels), `accent` (icons, links, buttons: Papa blue; #111111 in bw), `accent-text` (text on an accent background, e.g. the button label), `border`, `error-*`, `notice-*`, and per person slot `person-N` (marker, progress bar), `person-N-tint` (event card surface), `person-N-text` (text on the tint).
- bw only: `bw-event-card` (#E4E4E1, instead of the person tint), `bw-progress-fill` on `bw-progress-track` (instead of person colour on tint). In bw every person is #111111 and told apart by marker shape.
- Slots 1 to 4 are the spec's example family (1 Mama, 2 Papa, 3 Sebastian, 4 Mia). Slots 5 to 8 are derived the same way (similar lightness and saturation, tint = colour mixed 88 % with white, text = colour mixed 50 % with black) for additional people. The product owner decided on 15/11/2026 that a household holds up to **8 people (2 parents and 6 children)**, each with their own colour and their own bw shape, so there are 8 slots and 8 shapes. Slot 7 is an indigo (hue 237 degrees), slot 8 a raspberry rose (hue 346 degrees): both sit in hue gaps of the first six (blue 202, plum 309, terracotta 15) and keep at least 26 CIELAB units from every other slot. Colours and shapes repeat only from the 9th person on (the pair of colour and shape stays unique, ADR 0017); the person name next to the marker tells repeats apart.
- **Dark palette** (ADR 0015): warm near-black background and surface, light warm text, person colours lightened and muted, tint = person colour mixed 20 % into the surface, text on tint = person colour mixed 70 % with white. No shadows; separation is by surface and tint only.
- Contrast: text on every person tint, text on background and surface, and error and notice text reach at least 4.5:1 in all three palettes; person colours against the surface reach at least 3:1 (non-text); `accent` against background and surface and `accent-text` on `accent` reach 4.5:1 (accent is used as text).
- **Open decision (product owner, at preview approval):** the spec says person colours must also differ in lightness, but the spec's own light slots 1 and 3 are both L* 48 and the dark slots 1, 2 and 5 are L* 63 to 65 (3 and 6: 68). This is untested until decided; the shape in bw and the name next to the marker carry the distinction meanwhile.

## Typography

- Figtree, a variable font (weight 300 to 900, SIL OFL 1.1), served by the box from `/static/fonts/Figtree-wght.ttf` with its licence `OFL.txt` next to it. No outside font CDN. Fallback `system-ui, sans-serif`.
- Event titles are 600, in bw 700 (`--fw-event-title`). Labels are 14 px (`label`) or 15 px (`label-large`).

## Layout

- Outer margin 28 px; gap between columns and tiles 14 px; gap between header, main area and footer 20 px; column inner padding 16 px; event card padding 10 px by 12 px; gap between cards 10 px.
- Reference layout 1024 by 768 landscape: header, one white column per person in a 4-column grid, footer with two tiles (meal tile and tomorrow tile, ADR 0016; a tile without content is hidden and the other takes the full width, with both hidden the footer is hidden). Header: weekday, calendar week and date on the left; on the right two chips, the current time (hh:mm) and the weather. Each person column ends with a task progress bar and "done/total", anchored to the column bottom while the events scroll; a person without tasks has no bar.

- Family strip (ADR 0015) between header and columns: white cards (`.strip-item`) for whole-household events (no audience, or an audience that lists everyone: never repeated as one card per person; a timed one shows its time), all-day and multi-day events and public holidays (notice colours); a multi-day bar shows "Day n/m" with a flat edge where it continues. At most six person columns side by side in landscape and four in portrait (ADR 0016); they share the width and are never narrower than 144 px (`column-min`, the 150 px of ADR 0016 rounded down so that six fit 1024 px), beyond that they scroll sideways.

## Elevation

None. No shadows in any palette.

## Shapes

- Corners: columns and tiles 22 px, header chips 18 px, event cards 14 px, form fields 12 px, progress bar 4 px (height 8 px).
- Person marker: circle 14 px in the person colour. In bw a shape, 16 px. Markers appear only in the column header and the person chips in forms: they are the legend for black and white. Event cards and the family strip carry no markers; they say in words who the event is for (the who-line, Q62: "Whole family" in the web week, "with Kids", "everyone except Papa", else the names). The card tint carries the person in colour themes. Shapes in slot order: circle filled, circle empty, square filled, square empty, triangle filled, diamond filled, cross filled, star filled (`data-shape`). Slots 7 and 8 use solid forms with a clear silhouette (a thick plus with concave corners, a five-point star), no thin outlines, so they stay apart from the round, square and triangular shapes at 16 px in the column header and in the person chips; the empty circle and square are the only outline shapes and keep their 2 px ring.
- Journeys: no fill, 1.5 px dashed border in the person colour (bw: the person colour is #111111), label "Journey" after the time.
- Icons: line icons with 1.8 px stroke in the accent colour (Papa blue; bw #111111).

## Components

- `.tile`, `.column`, `.chip`: white surface cards with the radii above.
- `.event`: filled in the person tint, time (`.meta`) on top, title below, then the who-line (`.who`, meta size, wraps so the card grows; names are never cut, split or replaced by a count: each name with its comma is one `.nm` span (`display: inline-block`), the line wraps only between names, takes no automatic hyphens (`overflow-wrap: break-word; hyphens: manual`), and a name breaks only when wider than the card, and the column's own person is left out); `.event.journey` is the dashed variant. Person slot via `data-person`.
- Who-line rules, first match wins: everyone is "Whole family" (web week only: on the display an event for everyone goes to the family strip, like one with no audience); exactly one subgroup is "with <group>" (German "mit Gruppe <group>", correct for any group name); everyone except one or two is "everyone except <names>"; otherwise "with <names>". In the family strip the line follows the title in square brackets and never contains "with" or "mit": the bare group name for a subgroup ("[Kids]", "[Kinder]"), "everyone except <names>", or only the names; nothing for the whole family. On a card of the web week it sits under the time. Names are kept whole (see `.event`).
- `.progress`: person colour on person tint (bw #111111 on #D6D6D3).
- `.marker`: person marker as above.
- Tomorrow tile (`data-tile="tomorrow"`): label row `.tile-head` with the label and, from 18:00 with a collection, the pill `.flag` ("Put out tonight"; `accent` fill, black in bw; the `.alert` tile in bw has a 3 px border). Lines have a kind: `.ln.waste` (bin icon of 20 px before the title; collections that do not fit share one line, joined by ", ") and `.ln.note` (regular weight, below a thin rule; today's note next to a collection starts with a bold `.day` span "Today"). Lines wrap (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`) and are never clipped. The night page uses the same `.ln.waste`.
- Day note card on Home (`.note-day`): segmented control `.seg` (Today, Tomorrow preselected, Other day with a date field shown through `:has`), textarea with `.counter`, one primary button; planned notes as calm `.note-row` rows. Waste collection calendars show `.waste-next` under their switch.
- Web interface frame (`.frame`): brand bar (`.frame-bar`) with sign-out, navigation (`.frame-nav`, one `.chip`-shaped link per screen, at least 44 px high, current screen filled with `accent`), content (`main`). Under 900 px the navigation wraps above the content; from 900 px it stands as a column left of it. Without a `data-theme` the palette follows the device (`prefers-color-scheme`); an explicit `data-theme` wins. Screens a role may not use are not listed.
- Shared form patterns (ADR 0017, templates in `web/templates/patterns.html`): success line `.msg.ok` (white card, 1.5 px `accent` border, check icon, `role="status"`); field error `.ferr` under the field (`error-bg`, input with 2 px `error-text` border, `aria-invalid`); switch `.switch` (off: `text-secondary` outline, on: `accent` track) with its state in words; person chips `.pchip` (selected: person tint, 2 px person-colour border, tick, bold; bw: thick border and the marker shape; "Whole family" neutral and selected by default); segmented control `.seg` and weekday chips `.days` (selected: `accent`), choice cards `.choice` (selected: 2 px `accent` border); disclosure `details.more`; destructive actions only in `details.danger` on a detail page; empty state `.empty` with one sentence and the first action. All use existing tokens, at least 44 px targets.
- Time capsule `.tcap` (every time field, ticket 6.3 design pass): the time in a pill (`chip` radius, `background` fill, 16 px 600, tabular figures). On phones and tablets it is the system `input type="time"` and opens the system picker; on a computer and on displays it opens a picker panel `.tpick` below it: the chosen time in `key-figure`, hours 00 to 23 (12-hour format: 1 to 12 and an AM / PM `.seg`) and minutes in 5-minute steps as 44 px tap targets in a 6-column grid (selected: `accent`), "Done" as a secondary button. No wheel, no scrolling, no animation (mouse and E-Ink). An optional time ("none" allowed) is a `.switch` row whose state says what "no time" means, with the capsule shown only when on; never an empty time field.
- Meal plan (ticket 6.3): one `.meal-day` card per day (today: 3 px `accent` outline and a "Today" pill), one `.mrow` per mealtime in the order of the day: mealtime word with a line icon (sunrise, sun, apple, moon) in a label column, then the dish in a one-line text field that wraps long text instead of cutting it off (`textarea rows=1`, Enter saves). Inside a card narrower than 270 px the word sits above the dish. The week heading names the calendar week; week navigation uses bordered chip links with words and chevrons. A long form keeps its one primary action in a sticky `.savebar` at the bottom, which says "Changes not saved yet" once something is typed. The read view (children) shows dishes in `event-title`, leaves unplanned mealtimes out and says "Nothing planned." for an empty day.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do take every colour, size and radius from the tokens; do not hard-code values in templates.
- Do not rely on colour alone to tell people apart: bw and red-green colour deficiency need the marker shape or an icon.
- Do not add shadows, gradients or animations.
