## Context

ADR 0020 placed recipes (#193), star rewards (#194) and the box as address book (#195) in 0.3 and left their details to grilling rounds. The product owner decided them on 16/11/2026 (questions Q94 to Q136; the decisions are recorded on each ticket).

## Decision

### Recipes (#193)

- **Sources:** own entry, import from a web page, and a starter collection shipped with 0.3.
- **A recipe** has a name, pictures, base portions, ingredients with quantity and unit, numbered steps, an optional time, a source link, suitable mealtimes, a category, free tags, tips, a favourite mark per person, "last cooked" and, where available, nutrition values.
- **Own photos:** families add their own photos to any recipe, including imported and starter recipes, from the device's camera or gallery; one is the cover. Pictures follow the rules for article pictures (re-encoded, metadata removed), scaled to at most 1600 px.
- **Languages:** the starter collection exists in **German and English**, and the box shows the household's language. Recipes a family enters or imports keep the language they were written in.
- **Ingredients** are an article of the library, optionally with a **variety** ("Mehl · Type 405"), or free text. Allergies and diets are checked only for articles; free text carries "not checked automatically". Varieties also exist for list entries; quantities add up only for the same variety.
- **Units of an ingredient (product owner, 24/11/2026, ticket 279):** the household's units (ticket 165) plus two groups. *Kitchen measures* EL, TL, Prise, Messerspitze, Schuss, Spritzer, Handvoll and Tasse never convert into or add up with g and ml; on a shopping list the article goes on without a quantity. *Containers and piece measures* Dose, Päckchen, Becher, Bund, Glas, Flasche, Stange, Zehe, Scheibe, Würfel, Zweig, Stiel, Kugel, Blatt, Beet and Topf keep their quantity on a shopping list, and quantities add up only within the same unit. Reason: the starter collection uses them in 1 to 143 recipes each; without them about 250 ingredient lines would be free text and lose the allergy check.
- **Portions:** a meal plan entry defaults to the number of people in the household; quantities scale and are rounded to one decimal, never up to whole numbers.
- **Meal plan:** an entry is free text or a recipe; meal plan templates may hold recipes.
- **Meal plan entries with recipes (ticket 281, 24/11/2026, for the product owner to confirm):** an entry (typed by hand or a week's adjustment) holds free text or a recipe with portions from 1 to 99; the text of a recipe entry is the recipe's name, so a renamed recipe is renamed in the plan and in the calendar, a deleted recipe leaves its name as free text without portions (the delete box lists every planned day and mealtime from today on), and the calendar events, wall displays and Home show only the dish name. Without script a changed text that equals a recipe's name (ignoring case) becomes that recipe. Quantities below 0,1 show as 0,1, never 0. Meal plan templates still hold free text only: a template entry that is a recipe stays a later ticket.
- **Favourites and last cooked (ticket 287, 25/11/2026, for the product owner to confirm):** a favourite is a mark per person and recipe ("Mia likes it"); a parent sets it for any person of the household, a child sees it. Only own recipes carry it, since a starter recipe cannot be planned. The meal plan's suggestions put recipes somebody likes first, then those that fit the mealtime, then A to Z. "Last cooked" is not stored: it is the latest day of the plan with that recipe that is before today, so it follows the plan, also when the entry is later changed, and the "Finished cooking" button of the cooking view writes nothing. A recipe planned only for today or later has none.
- **Shopping list:** by hand (default; a tick list per meal or week) or automatically (household setting: every day at 03:00 the meals of the next 7 days, configurable). **Pantry articles** such as salt and oil are left out by default; nothing is added automatically in holiday mode. When a recipe leaves the plan or its portions shrink, its quantity is subtracted from entries not yet ticked.
- **Warnings** name the person and never block; vegetarian and vegan marks when all articles fit.
- **Cooking view** on every device (large text, ticking ingredients, step by step, screen stays on); wall displays show only the dish name. Parents create and plan recipes; children see them.
- **Import:** an own parser for schema.org/Recipe JSON-LD (make or buy: `go-recipe` used as reference only). The import takes everything for the household's private use and keeps the source link; imported recipes never leave the household.
- **Starter collection:** about 200 proven dishes from mixed established sources. Ingredients and quantities are taken over, the steps are written anew in own words, and each recipe names its source. A Sonnet agent researches the list and writes the texts; the product owner approves the list first. No invented recipes.
- **Starter collection as shipped (ticket 290, 24/11/2026):** the 200 approved files are embedded in the box (`internal/starter/data`, unchanged). They are not copied into each household: a starter recipe is built when it is shown, in the language of the person viewing it, read-only, with the id `starter-<slug>`, listed on its own page and at `GET /starter-recipes`, never in the family's own list. Unit spellings map to the recipe units (Pck., Pck, Packung and Päckchen to packet; Msp. to knife tip; Topf and Töpfchen to the new container unit pot; Stk and Stück to piece; l and Liter to l), a length such as "5 cm" ginger becomes one piece and centilitres become millilitres, so no ingredient is free text because of its unit. An ingredient is an article of the library when its German name is one (or starts with one, "Linsen, rot"); a line with an alternative ("Wasser oder Milch") stays free text so no allergen is missed. The note of a line stays behind the name ("Zwiebel (gehackt)").
- **Nutrition:** imported values from the source page; otherwise calculated from the ingredients with BLS 4.0 (Max Rubner-Institut, CC BY 4.0) and USDA FoodData Central (CC0), shipped offline. The recipe says the values are calculated and names the source; missing values show "incomplete". Seven values of the EU nutrition declaration per portion, as guide values, only in the recipe view, hidden for children unless a parent switches them on, never on wall displays.
- **MCP:** recipes belong to the meal plan area.

### Import from a web page (#289, 25/11/2026, for the product owner to confirm)

- **Two steps:** a parent gives a link (`/meals/recipes/import`); the box reads that one page and its picture through the connection "recipes" (the Gate; refused in offline mode or when switched off, 409 for the API), as a **draft** that belongs to the parent who read it and lives one hour. The parent checks the recipe form of #279, already filled in, and saves it. Only public names are read: no number address, nothing in the home network, also not after a redirect; at most 5 MB and 20 seconds; HTML only.
- **Reading:** the JSON-LD of the page (`Recipe`, also in `@graph` and lists; several recipes: the parent chooses one). Own parser, `golang.org/x/net/html` only for the tokenizer (a module the box already has). Quantities: ½ and other vulgar fractions, `1 1/2`, decimal comma, thousands point (`1.000`), a range takes the smaller number, "etwas" and 0 mean no quantity; a bracket that is only a quantity is dropped. Steps are plain text (HTML removed, entities decoded last), the name of a section starts its steps. A yield that is no portion count ("1 Blech (12 Stücke)") gives the number in brackets or the first number, a missing one the number of people, both marked "Bitte prüfen".
- **No silent default:** a line that is no library article is framed and asks what Hejmo should do (up to three proposals with their allergy warnings, an own article, free text); nothing is preselected, and saving without a choice is refused (422). An own article made this way has the default department, no allergens and, for lack of a safe default, the kind "vegetarian"; the recipe page names it with a link to add its allergens.
- **What stays:** the page's link is the source and is immutable (the store ignores a new source of an imported recipe, so the API does too); the picture is stored like article pictures at 1600 px as the cover; the nutrition values of the page are stored per portion, a missing value is never 0 (salt is sodium times 2.5). When ingredients or base portions change, the page's values stop to hold and the recipe falls back to the calculation of #288 and says who changed what (**for the product owner to confirm**).
- **Routes:** the pages of one recipe below `/meals/recipes/{id}/…` are one pattern (`{id}/{action}`), because `/meals/recipes/import/{id}` would otherwise conflict with each of them.

### Nutrition values (#288, product owner, 25/11/2026)

- **Data:** BLS 4.0 (7,140 foods, Max Rubner-Institut, CC BY 4.0) and USDA FoodData Central (Foundation Foods 2026-10-29 and SR Legacy 2018-04, CC0) ship as one extract of the seven values per 100 g and the names, `internal/nutrition/data/foods.tsv.gz` (about 0.5 MB), made by `tools/foodlist` from the files of the sources (never committed). A value the source does not give stays missing; for USDA the salt is sodium x 2.5, the carbohydrate is "by summation" or "by difference" less fibre, and a missing kJ or kcal is derived from the other (1 kcal = 4.184 kJ).
- **Every shipped article that is food maps to a food** (`internal/nutrition/data/articles.tsv`, a test fails for one without), with the weight of one piece and the density where Hejmo knows them. An own article gets an optional section "Nährwerte" in its form: a food search (offline, works without script), the chosen food and the weight of a piece. Without a food a recipe does not count the article and says so.
- **Units count in grams for nutrition only** (decision of the product owner, 25/11/2026): g, kg, oz and lb always; ml, l, US and UK measures, EL, TL and Prise (15 ml, 5 ml, 0.3 ml) with the article's density; Stück, Dutzend, Glas and Becher with the weight of a piece; everything else (Messerspitze, Schuss, Spritzer, Handvoll, Tasse, Dose, Zehe, Scheibe, Bund ...) has no weight. Shopping lists still never convert kitchen measures (see above).
- **Never 0 for unknown:** an ingredient that cannot be counted (free text, no quantity, a unit without weight, an own article without food) is listed by name with the reason; a value the food of a counted ingredient lacks is shown as "unvollständig" (API: `null`). With nothing countable the card says so and shows no numbers.
- **Visibility:** parents always, a child only when a parent switched on "Nährwerte bei Rezepten" on the child's person page (off at first, not available for adults); a child without it gets no card and no hint, and 404 from the API. Never in the recipe list, the cooking view, the meal plan or on displays. The recipe page names the sources with their licence, as CC BY 4.0 asks.

### Star rewards (#194)

- A **reward catalogue** per household; stars stay with the person. A reward has a name, emoji or picture, star price, who it is for and an optional limit.
- **Redemption:** a child asks, a parent confirms or declines; parents can redeem directly. Stars are booked only on confirmation and never go below zero.
- Details decided with #292 (product owner, 23/11/2026): a child's **open requests set their stars aside** (a new request needs the price in free stars); archiving or deleting a reward **declines** its open requests with the reason "reward gone" instead of blocking; limits apply to parents too; unticking a task whose stars were redeemed may take the balance **below zero**, switchable per household (on by default). `task_done.stars` holds the tick's full value, so every untick tries to deduct all of it; a re-tick that only pays off earlier debt books fewer stars or none, and writes no star-log line.
- One **savings goal** per person, with progress in the personal view and in the display's person column. Details decided with #293: progress is the **plain balance** on every screen (the child's goal card, the parents' star cards, the family and personal views, the display); open requests do not reduce it. The child's goal card offers "Ask parents" only with the price in free stars, a limit that is not used up and no open request for the goal; once asked it stays on the goal card with "Waiting for ..." and the request also stands under "Your requests". No catalogue or redeeming on wall displays.
- **Streaks** only for routines, shown as "this week 5 of 7" and best streak; **pause days** do not break them; a missed day gives no message. No automatic bonus stars.
- About ten shipped **badges**, never taken away; own badges later.
- A switch per person, on for children and off for adults by default. MCP: under the tasks area.

### Address book (#195)

- **Contacts:** the household's people plus others, each with a full name and one **Hejmo name**, several labelled phone numbers and e-mail addresses, address, birthday, photo, note, contact groups and **relations** to people.
- **CardDAV** for parents with a device token of the new area `contacts`; two-way, except that name and deleting of household people happen only in Hejmo. Hejmo is its own address book on the phone; contacts are taken over by vCard import, with duplicates offered for merging.
- **Children** get no CardDAV but an "Important numbers" view of contacts marked visible for children; a relation alone does not make a contact visible.
- A generated read-only calendar **Birthdays** and a layout block **Emergency numbers** (off by default).
- Contacts stay in the box; MCP only with the area `contacts`, no right by default.

## Consequences

- The data model grows by recipes, ingredients, varieties, nutrition data, rewards, redemptions, streaks, badges, contacts and relations.
- The box ships two nutrition databases and a starter collection with a credits file; the import of web pages is a further outbound request for the threat model (same rules as article pictures).
- Each 0.3 ticket is split into buildable tickets; Opus designs every screen before code.
